Privacy Policy

Tinnitus Relief: Sleep Sounds (package com.tinnitusrelief.tinnitus_relief), published by Triunity Engineering Limited. Last updated: 6 November 2026.

The short version

The app does not collect, send or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you set up stays on your phone.

What is stored on your phone

  • Your settings, saved mixes, the pitch you found, program progress and check-in answers.
  • An optional night log (when the app played, errors) that you can read in Settings and share yourself if you choose.

This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it. Settings also has a button to erase it.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is handled entirely by Google Play. Google processes the payment and records the purchase under your Google account. We never see your payment details, name or email. Google's own privacy policy applies to that process.

Permissions

  • Notifications and foreground service: to show the playback controls and keep sound playing with the screen off.
  • Wake lock and battery optimisation exemption: so night playback is not stopped by the phone.

The app does not use your microphone, camera, contacts, location or files.

Voices

Lesson voices are recordings that come inside the app. The optional "phone voice" uses your phone's own text-to-speech engine, which is part of Android and is not controlled by this app.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Not medical advice

This app offers sound-based relief and relaxation. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition. Keep volume at a comfortable level and see a doctor if tinnitus is sudden, one-sided, or comes with hearing loss or dizziness.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: use the developer contact shown on the app's Google Play listing.
