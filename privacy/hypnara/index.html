Hypnara privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026.

Short version: everything stays on your phone.

  • No account. You never sign up or log in.
  • No data collected. Your goals, sentences, voice recordings and ratings are stored only on your phone. We never receive them.
  • The microphone is used only while you are recording your own sentences in the app. Recordings are never uploaded or shared.
  • No ads and no tracking. The app has no advertising or analytics code.
  • Notifications are optional, created on your phone, and show one of your own sentences.
  • Home-screen widget: if you add it, one of your sentences is visible on your home screen.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We receive no payment details.
  • Deleting your data: use "Erase all my goals and recordings" in Settings, or delete the app. Both remove your data from the phone.
  • Children: Hypnara is intended for adults.
