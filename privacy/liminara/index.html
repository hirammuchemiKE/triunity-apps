Liminara: Privacy Policy

Liminara: Expand Consciousness, published by Triunity Engineering Limited. Last updated: 8 November 2026.

Short version: everything stays on your phone

Liminara has no account, no server, no ads and no analytics. We do not collect, store, share or sell any personal data.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your dream journal (text, tags and voice memos), remote viewing and ESP sessions, focus sessions, sound mixes and settings are stored only on your phone, inside the app's private storage. We never receive them.
  • Deleting the app, or using "Delete all my data" in Settings, removes all of it from your phone.

Permissions and why they are used

  • Microphone: only when you tap to record a voice memo of a dream. Recordings stay on your phone.
  • Notifications and alarms: to wake you gently at the time you chose, to play optional lucid-dream cues, and for optional daytime reality-check reminders.
  • Fingerprint / screen lock: to lock the Dream section and to confirm deleting or restoring data. Liminara never sees your fingerprint or PIN: Android checks it.
  • Battery optimisation exception: so Android does not stop the wake alarm overnight.

Backups and exports

A backup is a file you create, encrypted with a password only you know (PBKDF2 and AES-256). A CSV export is a plain file for your own spreadsheet. Where you save or send these files is your choice; we never receive them.

Purchases

The one-time full-version purchase is handled by Google Play. We receive no payment details.

Children

Liminara is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
