What it does

  • Your voice, your words — Pick a goal, tap starter lines or write your own, read each line aloud with big easy text. Every take is saved at once.
  • A session or a whole night — Now: 5, 10 or 20 minutes with a gentle wake-up. Sleep: 30 minutes, 1 or 2 hours, words fading to silence with the screen locked.
  • A real mixer — Your voice, the background and the beat, each on its own slider. Binaural for headphones, a pulsing tone for the speaker, or no beat at all.
  • Affirmations widget — Keep one of your sentences on the home screen, changing through the day.
  • Private by design — No account, no ads, no tracking. Recordings stay on the phone and are never uploaded; password-locked backup when you change phones.
  • 21-day chart — See how true each goal feels over three weeks, if you want the numbers.

The deal

Free and not stingy: unlimited recording, 3 goals, script mode, both session types, two sounds, the drowsy beat and the widget, no ads. One purchase unlocks more goals, all sounds, all-night repeat and script import. No subscription, ever.
