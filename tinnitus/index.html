What it does

  • Find your pitch — Slide a tone until it matches the ringing you hear. The app uses that pitch to suggest sounds and scenes.
  • Match or notch — Both work as a tinnitus masker: Match blends sound around your pitch, Notch dips the sounds right at your pitch. Compare both with one tap.
  • 29 sounds and a mixer — Rain, ocean, fan, white, pink and brown noise. Layer your own mixes or start from a scene.
  • Plays all night — One bedtime button: sound fades in, rotates if you want, and wakes you with birdsong instead of an alarm.
  • A 14-day program — A three-minute lesson a day on sleep, stress and attention — the habituation side, not a promise of a cure.
  • Private by design — No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on the phone.

The deal

Free to start: 8 sounds, 4 scenes, 3 of your own mixes and the first three program days. One purchase unlocks everything. No subscription, ever.
